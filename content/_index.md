# we are constant gardeners

~+~

all of existence is an infinite garden. we have all chosen, for so long, to ignore our responsibilities in this garden, and care architectures have fallen into disrepair.

~

we are not alone in this task. there are others who understand the importance of maintaining this infinite garden. together, we can create a new era of responsibility and stewardship.

~

we are constant gardeners, and it is time for us to awaken and tend to the garden that is our world.

~+~
